# Connect my WhatsApp number, and read its status

The Home screen is where you land when you sign in, and where the logo takes you back. It holds one thing: the
status of your WhatsApp account, and as long as no number is attached, the way to connect one.

**1. Open Meta’s window.** As long as no number is attached, a button on the Home screen opens Meta’s window. There
you choose your business account and your number, and everything else happens without you.

If you had already started once, starting again works: you have nothing to re-enter and no new code to request, you
find the existing account and number again. When Meta refuses, it is ITS reason that is shown (expired code,
account not shared, several numbers to choose between), not an opaque error message.

**2. No number? Get one.** The “No number? We provide one” link opens the “Connect WhatsApp” page: “Provide me a
number” offers you a dedicated UK number, included in the Pro plan, and €3.50 excl. VAT a month without it.

You pay on Stripe’s page (a promo code can be entered there, if we gave you one), then the number appears. Next, in
order:

- “Open the Meta window”;
- on the number screen, “Enter a new phone number”, the country GB (+44), then the digits that follow +44;
- verification by SMS (“Text message”) if Meta lets you choose;
- the code appears on the page within a few seconds, copy it into the window; with no code after 45 seconds, wait
  for Meta to offer to resend it and try the other method;
- finally, go all the way to Meta’s last screen (“Finish”), which completes the connection.

If Meta refuses this number, “Get another one” gives you a new one. “Manage my subscription”, on this page, opens
Stripe’s portal (card, invoices, cancellation); from Claude Code, ask Claude.

**3. Verify the number, if Meta asks.** Meta sometimes lets the sign-up finish without verifying the number: it is
yours, but it cannot send yet. The number card says so and carries the button that fixes it.

Meta sends you a code (by call, or by SMS if you prefer), you enter it, and the number is registered right away.
Meta only tolerates ten requests per number over 72 hours, all steps combined, and blocks the number beyond that:
the screen therefore refuses a second code within a minute and never retries on its own.

**4. Read the number’s status.** The number card shows your WhatsApp profile picture, the account status as a
coloured badge, the number’s quality, your display name and how many customers you can write to per 24 hours.

The profile picture is the one your recipients see: nothing appears until you have set one, which is the usual case
at the start. The customer count reads “Not yet evaluated by Meta” until Meta has decided. When a status cannot be
read, the screen says “Status unavailable” with a Retry button, rather than a reassuring green.

Just below, a **WhatsApp Business account** panel shows Meta’s review of the account, your business verification
and the owning business, each with its badge. Your payment method cannot be read from here: the screen sends you to
Meta’s Business Manager instead of showing an invented value.

**Four figures over 30 days** open the page (contacts, messages exchanged, templates sent, estimated cost), with the
Pro plan: the Free plan has no statistics. They are exactly those of the Performance Lab, and a dash replaces the cost
when Meta gives no rate.

**Good to know.** For your workspace’s first number, €1 of AI credit is offered as soon as Meta has verified it, only
once: at connection if it already is, otherwise when you activate it with the verification code. It pays for your AI
agents, and for translation and transcription in the Inbox (see the AI credit tab of Settings > Plan).

**One workspace runs one number.** Connecting a second one is refused, telling you which one is already there and
what to do: create a second workspace, or detach this one. Accepting both would mix up the conversations of the same
person.
