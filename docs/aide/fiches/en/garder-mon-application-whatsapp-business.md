# Keep my WhatsApp Business app

Is your number already used in the WhatsApp Business app on your phone? Keep it: you keep replying there, and Messaging
Me sends the messages of your website, your software or your store. No Meta Business account? Meta’s window has you
create it along the way.

**1. Open “Keep my WhatsApp Business app”.** Ask Claude Code: it gives you a link to the “Connect WhatsApp” page. In
the console, the same button is on the Home screen. The page sums up what changes, then the button opens Meta’s
window, where you sign in with your Facebook profile.

**2. Enter your number.** Enter the number of your WhatsApp Business app, with its country, then choose how Meta
verifies it (by text message or by phone call) and follow what it asks.

**3. Check your profile.** Meta recognizes your app’s profile: its name, its picture and your number. If it does not
match, go back and fix the number.

**4. Choose or create your Meta Business account.** If you already have a business portfolio, choose it. Otherwise,
choose “Create a business portfolio”: it is created along the way.

**5. Describe your business.** For a new portfolio, Meta asks for your business name, an email address, the country
and your website.

**6. Link your app.** In the WhatsApp Business app on your phone, scan the QR code the window shows. If you cannot
scan it, the window offers an access code: a message from the official Facebook business account arrives in the app,
tap “Connect”, then enter that code. Messaging Me does not take over your contacts or past conversations: they stay
in the app.

**7. Confirm your WhatsApp Business account.** Check its name and time zone, then continue.

**8. Accept sharing with Messaging Me.** Meta sums up what Messaging Me will be able to do: manage your WhatsApp
account, its conversations, and pass your account’s events on to Meta. Confirm.

**9. Finish.** “Your account is connected to Messaging Me”: your WhatsApp account is created, and your portfolio if it
is new. Click “Finish”; the payment method at Meta can wait for your first template. Your number shows on the Home
screen. If not, start “Keep my WhatsApp Business app” again: the window picks up what is already created and completes
the connection.

## What changes in the app

The messages you type there stay free; those sent from Messaging Me are billed by Meta. The app loses its broadcast
lists, its catalog, its away message and its labels, and its linked devices are unlinked. Open it at least every two
weeks.

What you type in the app does not show in Messaging Me yet. If an AI agent answers your customers, it does not see
your replies: it may answer at the same time as you.

**Good to know.** Meta can refuse a number that is too new in the app (“More activity on the WhatsApp Business App is
needed”): use the app for a few days, then try again.
