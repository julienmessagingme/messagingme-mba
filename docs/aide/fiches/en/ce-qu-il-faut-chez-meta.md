# What you need at Meta, before and after connecting

WhatsApp goes through Meta: your WhatsApp account, your number and the billing of messages live with them. Here is
what you need, in order. The “Connect WhatsApp” page and Claude tell you exactly the same thing.

**1. A Meta Business account.** A Meta Business account, that is a business portfolio (formerly Business Manager):
it will hold your WhatsApp account, your number and your card. You sign in with your Facebook profile, or with a
managed Meta account from your organization.

**2. A portfolio, if you do not have one yet.** Create it on business.facebook.com, before connecting your number.

No portfolio yet? On business.facebook.com, sign in, open the menu under “Home” at the top left, then create a
business portfolio: your business’s public name (no special characters), your first name, last name and a business
email address, then confirm the email Meta sends you. Meta’s window can also create it along the way, but doing it
first is safer.

**3. A spare number.** A number that is not used in the WhatsApp app (nor WhatsApp Business): once connected, it
leaves the app. No spare number? We provide one.

**4. Right after connecting, your card at Meta.** Meta bills your business directly, not Messaging Me: add your card
to your portfolio, as follows.

- On business.facebook.com, open WhatsApp Manager, “Overview” page.
- On your WhatsApp account, click the three dots, then “Manage account settings”.
- In the “Settings” tab, open “Payment settings”, then “Add payment method”.
- Enter a Visa or Mastercard card (Meta accepts neither American Express nor PayPal), then your business details;
  Meta may ask for your tax information.
- You must own the WhatsApp account, or have the “Manage WhatsApp account” permission. Without a valid card, only
  replies within the 24-hour window go out: templates (campaigns, follow-ups) are blocked.

**Good to know.** As long as Meta reports the payment method as missing or failing, an “Add your card at Meta”
banner stays on the Home screen, with these steps. It disappears on its own once the card is in place, usually
within 20 minutes, or as soon as you reopen the Home screen.
