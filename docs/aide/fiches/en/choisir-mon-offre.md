# Choose my plan

Your workspace has a plan: **Free**, **Pro** (a subscription, monthly or yearly), **Business** (monthly, set up by
us) or **Enterprise** (on quote). It decides which features are open, and the limits on what you can create or send.

**1. See where you stand.** The Settings > Plan menu shows your plan, how much of each limit your workspace uses
(contacts, templates sent this month, automations switched on, users), and the plans side by side. Outside Free, its
second tab, AI credit, tops up the prepaid credit of your AI agents.

**2. Upgrade to Pro.** On the Plan page, choose “Monthly” or “Yearly”: you pay on Stripe’s secure page, where a
promo code can be entered, and your workspace switches to Pro as soon as the payment is confirmed, usually within the
minute.

If your workspace has never had Pro, it starts with a free 14-day trial: the card is requested, nothing is charged
before the trial ends, and the Plan page shows the end date. If these buttons do not appear, “Upgrade to Pro” opens
Support with the subject already filled in. Business and Enterprise are requested through Support, with “Contact us”
on the Plan page: we set the plan on your workspace, with no online payment, and a 14-day Business trial is available
on request.

**3. Follow and cancel my subscription.** On Pro, the “Your Pro subscription” card, at the top of the Plan page, shows
its status (active, free trial, payment overdue or cancellation scheduled), its billing and price, its start date, and
its next renewal or its end date.

“Cancel my subscription” stops Pro at the end of the paid period, or of the trial
without charging anything: the confirmation gives the exact date, and until that date “Keep my subscription” keeps it.
“Change card” opens Stripe’s secure page.

To switch from monthly to yearly, write to us through Support. A cancellation refunds nothing: your workspace keeps Pro
until the end date, then goes back to Free.

**4. Find my invoices.** The Plan page lists your workspace’s invoices, for the subscription, the number and the AI
credit top-ups, with their amount and status. Each one opens on Stripe’s page, with its PDF.

## What each plan includes

**What the Free plan includes.** Your WhatsApp number, the API and the connection to Claude, with caps: contacts,
templates sent each month, a single user. Claude and the API read your conversations, reply to your
customers within 24 hours, send your templates and build your AI agent. In the console, Free keeps the Home screen
(your number), the API keys, the documentation, the plan, your account and support.

**What Pro, Business and Enterprise add.** Pro opens the console: the Inbox, scenarios, statistics and email, with
more contacts. It includes three users, all administrators: beyond that, each extra user is paid for, and its price is
shown when you invite the member, before anything is billed. The Plan page removes one, without a refund, once the
extra member is deactivated. Business adds the Meta Business Agent, the setup assistants, the help assistant, the
customisable widget and team roles (administrator, manager, agent), with five users, onboarding and priority support;
an extra user is requested through Support. Ads, channels, RCS, Salesforce and Batch, and the full Performance Lab
require Enterprise.

**What the contact limit counts.** On Free, all your contacts count, including those who write to you first. When
your Free workspace is full, creating or importing a contact is refused, and a new customer who writes to you does not
get into Messaging Me: their message is not recorded and your AI agent does not reply to them. An email lets you know,
at most once a day. Your existing contacts write to you as before, and a deleted contact frees its place: ask Claude to
delete the ones you no longer need. Outside Free, a contact who writes to you first is never counted nor refused.

**What is never counted.** A reply sent within 24 hours of a customer’s message is not a template send: it is not
counted.

**When something is not in your plan.** The menu stays visible, greyed out, with a padlock: it takes you to the Plan
page. If an action is refused because a limit is reached, a message tells you so, with a “See plans” button.

**How long your conversations are kept.** 30 days on Free, 60 days on Pro, 90 days on Business; on Enterprise, the
duration agreed with you. A shorter duration only applies 30 days after entering the plan (when the workspace is
created, when your Pro ends, when you leave Business or Enterprise): changing plan deletes nothing on the spot.

## If your workspace goes back to Free

Nothing is deleted, and everything comes back when you take Pro again. Meanwhile, only your 10 oldest automations
still start their scenarios (your widgets no longer start theirs), Meta’s agent no longer takes new conversations,
and members beyond the Free plan’s limit have their access suspended (the oldest administrator keeps theirs): as
soon as the workspace is back on Pro, “Retry” gives them the console back. All your contacts stay, even beyond the
Free plan’s limit: as long as there are too many, you can no longer create or import any, and a new customer who
writes to you does not get in.

An email, the Plan page and Claude tell you how many to delete to get back to the limit. Ask Claude to delete them, in
one go, and a few more to let new customers in: the oldest, or those with a tag. It first shows you how many there are
and a few records, and only deletes once you agree. As long as your workspace is above its limit, these deletions are
not capped per day.

## Your provided number

It is included in Pro, Business and Enterprise: get it at no cost from the “Connect WhatsApp” page. If it was already paid for separately,
that subscription stops at the first Pro payment, with a credit on the next invoice. If you cancel Pro, the Plan page
and an email warn you: when Pro ends, the number moves to €3.50 excl. VAT a month on the same card. To give it back
instead, choose “Give back my number when Pro ends” on the Plan page; you can change your mind as long as Pro is
running. If you send through your own number, the provided number does not resume: it is released seven days after
Pro ends.
