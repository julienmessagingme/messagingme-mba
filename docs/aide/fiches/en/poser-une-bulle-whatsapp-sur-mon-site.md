# Put a WhatsApp bubble on my site

The WhatsApp widget is a bubble you put on your site. The visitor clicks, WhatsApp opens with a message already
written, and they send it: they speak first, so the conversation opens without an approved template, and it lands
in your Inbox.

On a phone, the bubble opens WhatsApp directly; on a computer, it shows a QR code to scan.

**Create the widget.** Give it a name (to find your way around, the visitor does not see it), the pre-filled
message, who answers, and its look: a color, one of the four corners of the screen, a short label and an avatar if
you wish (the look is customisable on Business and Enterprise; elsewhere, the bubble keeps the standard WhatsApp
look). A preview shows the bubble as it will appear. For the visitor, the bubble speaks the language of their
browser, French or English; your label stays as you wrote it. Then copy the tag and paste it on every page where
the bubble should appear. If our service does not answer, the bubble simply does not appear: your page is never
slowed down.

**The pre-filled message tells where the conversation comes from.** It must be unique to this widget: it can
neither contain nor be contained in the message of another widget or of a channel link, and a message that already
appears in your conversations is refused, with their number. Conversations that came through your widgets do not
count: you can reuse the message of a deleted widget, or shorten the message of a widget. Case, accents and final
punctuation do not tell two messages apart. Finally, the message cannot start with a stop word (“stop”,
“unsubscribe”…): it would be read as an unsubscribe request, and every visitor would be unsubscribed.

**Who answers.** By default, like the other conversations. You can also hand the widget’s conversations to the
automatic responder (the one you chose on the Home screen, in “Who answers the customer”), or start a scenario,
provided it is published: a scenario that was never published would start nothing, so publish it first. For an AI
agent to answer the widget’s visitors, choose it in “Who answers the customer”. If the chosen scenario is deleted,
the widget no longer starts anything: its record says so, choose another one.

**Turn off or delete.** When off, the bubble disappears but the tag can stay on your site; you turn it back on in
one move. You can create five widgets at most. With no WhatsApp number linked, the bubble shows greyed out and does
not open.

**From an assistant.** An assistant such as Claude Code, plugged into your workspace through the MCP server
(Developers menu), can also list, create and edit your widgets, with the same rules, and paste the tag on your site
itself.
