# HubSpot on the Home screen: connect it, follow it, cut it off

When HubSpot is linked to your workspace, the Home screen gives it its own block. It is connected and set up in
Settings, even before you have a WhatsApp number.

**HubSpot**, when linked, has its own block: the portal, the sync status and a link to the setup guide. Cutting it
off makes you choose between a **pause**, which can be undone (the analyses produced during the pause are sent when
it resumes), and a **full disconnection**, which unlinks the HubSpot account and cuts off every number of the
workspace. Just below, the block says whether your HubSpot lists serve as campaign audiences, with a link to the
setting.

**HubSpot is connected and set up in Tools > Integrations**, with or without a WhatsApp number: a brand new workspace
can therefore connect HubSpot before it has a number. When you come back from connecting, the screen asks what you
plug in: the contact record and the analysis of every conversation, the immediate task when a customer asks for a
human, the creation of tasks (otherwise properties only), the unreachable contact, replying to customers from
HubSpot, your lists as campaign audiences. Each choice can then be changed with a switch.

**Replying to customers from HubSpot** is off at first; it needs a linked HubSpot portal, which the Business and
Enterprise plans allow. Once on, anyone in the
portal reads the conversation and replies from the Messaging Me card of the contact record, signed with their
address, as long as the 24-hour window is open; “Hand back to the bot” gives control back to the agent. The card
never writes to a contact who said STOP or to a blocked contact. A conversation held by a member can only be answered
by them, an admin or a manager, and each person sends at most 20 replies a minute.

**The “Messaging Me” page in HubSpot** (the Marketplace icon, then the app) lists the conversations waiting for a
person: urgent ones first, then the longest wait. “Open” shows the conversation so you can reply, as from the card.
Anyone on the portal sees the whole list, whatever their rights on contacts in HubSpot. If the number's sync is
paused, the page says so.

**Sending templates from HubSpot** can be turned on once replying is on. When the customer has not written for 24
hours, the card offers your approved templates, with their variables already filled in from the contact record: you
check them, then “Send the template”. A Marketing template only goes to a contact who has agreed; the other
templates go to any contact who has not said STOP and is not blocked. Meta charges for each template, and each person
sends at most 5 a minute.

**HubSpot workflows: send WhatsApp templates** is off to begin with; it requires the plan that links HubSpot, and does
not depend on replying. The “Send a WhatsApp template (Messaging Me)” action shows up in your HubSpot contact
workflows, but it only sends once this line is on (while it is off, the workflow history says workflows are not
enabled). There you choose the workspace, an approved Utility template, the property holding the contact's WhatsApp
number and, for each template variable, the property that fills it (fixed text lives in the template). Nothing goes to a contact
who said STOP or is blocked, nor to a landline, nor the same template to the same contact twice within 24 hours;
Marketing templates are not offered. The workflow history says, contact by contact, what was sent or why nothing was
sent, and each send shows up in Campaigns. At most 60 sends a minute and 2,000 a day for the workspace; Meta charges for
each template.

With no portal linked, the Home screen only shows a line that sends you there. To unlink HubSpot, do the full disconnection from the HubSpot block (with no number,
it is a button of the block, without the pause option).
