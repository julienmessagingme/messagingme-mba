Developers Free, no subscription WhatsApp in your app, in one Claude Code session.

Your WhatsApp number, an AI agent that answers your customers and a chat bubble on your site: Claude Code sets it all up from your terminal.

  • No account to create

Three steps, one session.

  1. 1

    Install the plugin

    In Claude Code, one after the other. No account to create, no key to copy.

    /plugin marketplace add julienmessagingme/messagingme-plugin
    /plugin install messagingme@messagingme
  2. 2

    Authorize

    Type /mcp, pick plugin:messagingme:messagingme, then “Continue with Google” on the page that opens. No account yet? Your workspace is created right then, with no form. One click on “Authorize”, and Claude takes it from there.

  3. 3

    Start

    Claude reads where your workspace stands, then takes you to your WhatsApp number: your own, or one we provide. That is when Meta asks for your free Meta Business account, which its window has you create along the way if you lack one (the step-by-step guide). Then an AI agent that answers your customers and a bubble on your site. An interrupted session resumes where it stopped.

    /messagingme:demarrer

Cursor, VS Code or Lovable: add the MCP server https://api.messagingme.app/mcp, and the same authorization opens.

What you can build.

Each line leads to its page: the use case with its film and its code, or the documentation.

Answer your customers

  • An AI agent that answers day and night from your site or your docs, and hands over to your team when needed.
  • The WhatsApp bubble on your site, Lovable included: the visitor writes, the agent answers.
  • Appointment booking in the conversation: the agent notes the reason, your app offers the slots.
  • Your conversations read and answered from Claude, within the 24-hour window or with a template.

Message them at the right time

  • Order tracking when it ships, and the AI agent if the customer replies.
  • The reminder the day before the appointment: the customer confirms or reschedules with one button.
  • The welcome message at sign-up, with a button to the first use of your product.
  • The failed payment reminder, with the link to update the card.
  • A campaign to your contacts, prepared by Claude and launched with your yes (Pro plan).

Connect it to your app

  • Every message received, delivered, read or failed, pushed to your app by a signed webhook.
  • Your sends through the API or the TypeScript SDK, safe to retry without duplicates thanks to the idempotency key.
  • Your contacts and their consent, kept up to date from your app.

Frequently asked questions.

Is this the official WhatsApp API?

With a Meta Business account, yes: Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform. Messaging Me is a Meta Tech Provider, and the WhatsApp Business account lives in your Business Manager and stays yours. Without a Meta Business account, you can link the WhatsApp Business app on your phone: it goes through WhatsApp as a linked device, it is not the official API.

I have no Meta Business account: can I still use it?

Yes, in two ways. Meta’s window has you create your Meta Business account along the way, with your Facebook profile, and your number goes through the official API. Or link the WhatsApp Business app on your phone with a QR code, with no account at all (no Facebook profile, no card at Meta): the AI agent answers the customers who write, without Meta billing those replies, Claude reads and replies there, and you can still reply from the phone. It goes through WhatsApp as a linked device, it is not the official API. Nothing goes out first there (no templates, campaigns or Shopify messages), and WhatsApp can cut a number. Later, to write first, this number keeps the app and goes through Meta, keeping its conversations: Meta’s window then creates the Meta Business account. The Pro plan needs the API.

API number or WhatsApp Business app: which one should I choose?

It depends on what your number must do. An official API number, connected through Meta, writes first. It sends templates, confirmations, reminders, campaigns and Shopify messages; it needs a Meta Business account, free, which Meta’s window has you create along the way with your Facebook profile, and a payment method at Meta, which bills the templates and, beyond a monthly allowance, the replies. Without a Meta Business account, for a separate number, the WhatsApp Business app linked by QR code needs no account, Meta does not bill its replies and it stays on your phone, but it only answers those who write to you. You can also keep your WhatsApp Business app on the same number while going through the API, if you want: it is also the next step of a number linked by QR code that wants to write first, conversations included. Without a free number, a provided number costs €3.50 excl. VAT a month, included in Pro. Claude asks you the questions one at a time during setup.

What do I need at Meta before I start?

1) A Meta Business account, that is a business portfolio (formerly Business Manager): it will hold your WhatsApp account, your number and your card. You sign in with your Facebook profile, or with a managed Meta account from your organization. 2) No portfolio yet? Nothing to prepare: Meta’s window has you create it along the way, with your Facebook profile (your business name, an email address, the country and the website), then offers a payment method. You can also create it beforehand on business.facebook.com: open the menu under “Home” at the top left, then create a business portfolio. 3) A number that is not used in the WhatsApp app (nor WhatsApp Business): once connected, it leaves the app. No spare number? We provide one.

My business name shows up wrong on WhatsApp.

That is the WhatsApp display name, separate from your workspace name. You change it in your Business Manager, and Meta reviews every change: during the review, or after a rejection, WhatsApp may show the number instead.

My message was not sent.

After 24 hours without a message from the person, only a template approved by Meta can be sent (send_template_to_contact). Within 24 hours of their last message, a free-form reply goes out (reply_in_open_window). get_conversation tells whether the window is open.

Why does Meta ask for a card, and what does it charge?

Meta bills your business directly, on the payment method of your Business Manager: templates, and since October 1, 2026 replies within the 24-hour window too, according to its rate card and the person’s country. Messaging Me never touches these fees. Meta’s rate card: developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/pricing. To add the card: 1) On business.facebook.com, open WhatsApp Manager, “Overview” page. 2) On your WhatsApp account, click the three dots, then “Manage account settings”. 3) In the “Settings” tab, open “Payment settings”, then “Add payment method”. 4) Enter a Visa or Mastercard card (Meta accepts neither American Express nor PayPal), then your business details; Meta may ask for your tax information. 5) You must own the WhatsApp account, or have the “Manage WhatsApp account” permission. Without a valid card, only replies within the 24-hour window go out: templates (campaigns, follow-ups) are blocked.

My template was rejected.

Claude reads the rejection reason (get_template_status), suggests a corrected version and submits it again (create_template). Meta may also change a template’s category: list_templates shows the one that applies.

My AI agent stopped answering.

Four causes. The AI credit is used up: get_credit, then buy_credit. The agent was deactivated, or is no longer the one answering: get_setup_state tells. Someone replied by hand, Claude included (reply_in_open_window): replying takes over the conversation, and the agent stays silent until it gets it back, on its own after a delay without a new reply, or right away: control_conversation hands it back from Claude, or “Hand back” in the console Inbox. And on Free, a new customer who writes to a workspace that already has its 100 contacts does not get in, so the agent does not answer them: get_plan shows the count.

Can my AI agent act in my application?

Yes, through an action: set_agent_action adds it to the agent, with an instruction (when to trigger it, what to tell the customer next) and what it collects (a contract number, a date, a reason), all optional. When the customer makes the request, the agent answers what the instruction says and carries on, and your application receives the agent.action webhook, at most once a day per contact and per action. This type is unchecked by default: add it to the address that already serves your application (update_webhook_endpoint, or the console, Developers > Webhooks sortants), or create one (create_webhook_endpoint). Without an application, a console automation can start from the action.

The verification code does not arrive.

With a number provided by Messaging Me, the code is captured for you and Claude shows it (watch_whatsapp_connection). With your own number, ask for a call rather than an SMS when Meta offers the choice. Meta allows only ten code requests per number over 72 hours.

Can I keep this number in the WhatsApp app?

Yes, with the WhatsApp Business app: the same number stays in the app on your phone and also goes through the API, if you want (“Keep my WhatsApp Business app”, from Claude or the console’s Home). You keep replying from your phone, but what you type there does not show in Messaging Me yet. A few app features stop (broadcast lists, away message), and you need to open the app at least every two weeks. With the regular WhatsApp app, the number leaves it when it goes through the API: then use a dedicated number, your own or a provided one.

Do I own the provided number?

No: it is rented for €3.50 excl. VAT a month, included in Pro. A number you bring stays yours.

How many messages can I send?

Three limits. The plan’s: 1,000 templates a month on Free, unlimited on Pro; replies within the 24-hour window never count. The API’s: a daily quota per workspace, set by the plan, where templates and replies are counted separately (sending templates never takes the room of replies), raised on request; beyond it, the quota_exceeded refusal until the next day. And Meta’s, which sets what a number can send and raises it with its quality.

What happens when my Free workspace reaches 100 contacts?

On Free, all your contacts count, those who write to you first included. Once the workspace is full, creating or importing a contact is refused (plan_limit_reached), and a new customer who writes to you does not get in: no record, no conversation, no reply from your AI agent. An email lets you know, one a day at most. If that customer wrote STOP, their refusal is still kept. Your existing contacts write and receive as before. To make room, ask Claude to delete the contacts you no longer need, in bulk: the oldest, those with a tag, by consent or from a list (prepare_contacts_deletion, then delete_contacts). It first shows you how many and a few records, then deletes on your go-ahead, 1,000 at most at a time; the deletion is permanent. Or move to Pro, where those who write to you first do not count and are never turned away.

Can Claude prepare and launch a campaign?

Yes, on Pro, and nothing goes out without your yes. Claude finds the contacts (find_contacts), turns them into a segment, which is a tag (tag_contacts), then prepares the campaign (prepare_campaign): a draft visible in the console, with a summary (contacts targeted, how many will receive it, those left out and why, 5 names, the number of messages Meta bills) and a code valid for 30 minutes. It only launches (launch_campaign) after your yes, with that code; a chosen date schedules the send. You can also launch the draft from the console. The template must be approved, and a marketing template only reaches contacts who opted in.

How do I add a colleague?

From the console, with an invitation. 1 user on Free, 3 on Pro then €15 excl. VAT per extra user per month (offered when inviting), per quote on Enterprise.

How do I cancel, and what happens to the number?

Pro and the provided number are managed in the Stripe portal, from the console Plan page or through Claude (manage_number_subscription). When Pro ends, the provided number moves to €3.50 excl. VAT a month on the same card, unless you choose to give it back (return_number_at_plan_end).

How do I update the plugin?

It does not update on its own. In Claude Code: /plugin, Marketplaces tab, messagingme, “Update marketplace” (or “Enable auto-update” from then on). In a terminal: claude plugin update messagingme@messagingme; on Windows, in PowerShell, type claude.cmd instead of claude (PowerShell blocks the claude.ps1 script). The new code loads in the next session, or after /reload-plugins.

I had already added the server by hand: what now?

Connected without a key (authorized through /mcp): nothing to do. Claude Code keeps yours and sets the plugin’s aside, with no duplicate tools. Connected with an API key: remove it (claude mcp remove followed by its name). A key does not open the setup tools, and yet Claude Code would keep that server.
